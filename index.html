The problem

Most automation fails at step one.

Someone automates the step they noticed, not the step that costs the most.

The result is a clever tool nobody uses, while the real time sink, the copying, chasing and re-typing that fills each week, carries on by hand. We start by looking at the whole workflow, so the first thing we build is the thing that pays back fastest.

What we build

Three kinds of work, one goal: fewer hours on repeat.

Workflow automations

Connect the tools you already use so data moves on its own.

  • Form and email enquiries into your job list or CRM
  • Invoices, reminders and follow-ups sent on schedule
  • Reports that build themselves each week

AI agents

Assistants that handle the first pass of routine conversations and paperwork.

  • Reply drafts for common customer questions
  • Reading documents and pulling out the details
  • Qualifying leads before they reach you

CRM setup

One place for every lead, client and job, set up the way your team works.

  • Pipelines that match your real sales steps
  • Clean import of your existing contacts
  • Training so the team actually uses it
